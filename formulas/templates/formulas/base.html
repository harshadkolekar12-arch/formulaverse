Bernoulli's Principle Fluid Mechanics
P + ½ρv² + ρgh = const

Governs energy conservation for ideal fluids. Explains lift forces in aerodynamics and fluid pressure changes in venture tubes.
Lorentz Force Law Electromagnetism
F = q(E + v × B)

Calculates combined electric and magnetic forces on moving charges. Core concept for cyclotrons and mass spectrometers.
Stokes' Drag Law Viscosity
F_d = 6π η r v

Determines viscous drag on spherical objects in laminar fluid flow. Crucial for Millikan oil drop calculations.
